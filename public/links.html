$CR And Platinum

Cryptic Realm stays free to play. The $CR economy direction is opt-in: wallet ownership or future approved staking can unlock premium platinum earning privileges without blocking ordinary players from entering the world.

Platinum is the in-game bridge currency for limited cosmetics, houses, mounts, creator marketplace items, and Exchange realm trades. A future marketplace can let players buy or sell approved platinum-denominated listings for $CR after rules, fraud controls, and compliance are finished.

Wager/casino-style mechanics are not live and should not ship until legal and platform review is complete. $CR is a game utility token, not an investment promise.
