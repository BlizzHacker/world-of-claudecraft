Summary

Cryptic Realm expands a classic-MMO browser engine with realm identity, custom visuals, player options, mini-games, moderation tools, recoverable operations, and a private roadmap for the independent game.

The token layer is optional. The game must remain playable without a wallet, seed phrase, token balance, or purchase.

Product Pillars

  • Free play first: ordinary gameplay, account creation, and realm entry stay available without $CR.
  • Realm identity: shared engine improvements can coexist with Cryptic Realm-specific lore, visuals, rules, and economy policy.
  • Customization: camera modes, HUD skins, realm skins, mini-games, wallet status, and future mod toggles belong in the in-game Customization menu.
  • Operational trust: admin, moderation, auto-update, proof links, and backups should be visible, auditable, and recoverable.

$CR And Platinum Utility

$CR is intended as an optional game utility token for supporting the realm, proving ownership, accessing premium utility, and powering future marketplace settlement. Platinum is the in-game premium bridge currency.

Players who hold or later stake an approved amount of $CR may receive privileges such as platinum earning eligibility, premium event access, cosmetic claim windows, housing or mount claim access, and marketplace listing tools. Players without $CR can still play the game normally.

Platinum can be used for limited cosmetics, houses, mounts, creator marketplace items, and Exchange realm trades. Future marketplace work may allow approved platinum-denominated listings to clear in $CR once the fraud controls, tax/accounting posture, and legal review are ready.

Game Economy Guardrails

  • No wallet or token balance should be required to play the base game.
  • Premium utility should be explicit: cosmetic access, optional platinum earning, account-linked achievements, and approved marketplace features.
  • Wager, casino, or gambling-style systems are not live and should not ship without legal, payment, age-gating, and platform review.
  • The Exchange realm is the natural home for cross-realm auctions, local trades, and $CR-priced listings.

Custody And Recovery

Cryptic Realm has two critical custody layers: the Phantom wallet recovery phrase/private keys, and the mint authority keypair used to control token minting.

The detailed custody runbook is admin-only. Public pages should prove the token exists, but they should not publish operational backup procedures or invite players to send private material anywhere.

Roadmap

  1. Stabilize live realm deployment, mobile performance, and realm routing.
  2. Complete the Customization menu for camera, HUD, realm skin, mini-games, wallet, and future mod toggles.
  3. Restore original Cryptic Realm mini-games and assets through an optimized asset pipeline.
  4. Harden admin-only custody backups across offline USB media and trusted hosts.
  5. Split upstream-safe improvements into separate ClaudeCraft pull requests.
  6. Publish clear economy rules before any meaningful $CR supply is minted or distributed.

Disclaimers

Cryptic Realm tokens and items are utility records for game systems and community features. $CR is not an investment promise; it is not a security, share, investment contract, revenue claim, staking product with promised returns, or promise of price appreciation.
