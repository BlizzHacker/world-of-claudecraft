Summary

Cryptic Realm expands the World of ClaudeCraft engine with realm identity, custom visuals, player options, mini-games, moderation tools, recoverable operations, and a private roadmap for Wade's independent game.

The token layer is optional. The game must remain playable without a wallet.

Product Pillars

  • Play first: wallet features must not block ordinary gameplay.
  • Realm identity: shared engine improvements can coexist with Cryptic Realm-specific lore, visuals, rules, and economy policy.
  • Customization: camera modes, HUD skins, realm skins, mini-games, and future mod toggles belong in the in-game Customization menu.
  • Operations transparency: admin, moderation, auto-update, and backup systems should be boring, auditable, and recoverable.

Token Utility

The Cryptic Realm SPL token is intended for gameplay achievement claims, cosmetic and collectible ownership records, account-linked event rewards, and future item receipts where an on-chain record is useful.

Custody And Recovery

Cryptic Realm has two critical custody layers: the Phantom wallet recovery phrase/private keys, and the mint authority keypair used to control token minting.

Both must be backed up offline. Server copies are hot keys and should not be the only source of truth. Long-term hardening should move authority to a hardware wallet or multisig, then optionally revoke authority after tokenomics are fixed.

Roadmap

  1. Stabilize live realm deployment, mobile performance, and realm routing.
  2. Complete the Customization menu for camera, HUD, realm skin, mini-games, wallet, and future mod toggles.
  3. Restore original Cryptic Realm mini-games and assets through an optimized asset pipeline.
  4. Harden backups across Slimmm, Thiccc, and Wade's Windows workstation.
  5. Split upstream-safe improvements into separate Claudcraft pull requests.
  6. Publish clearer economy rules before any meaningful token supply is minted.

Disclaimers

Cryptic Realm tokens and items are utility records for game systems and community features. They are not securities, shares, investment contracts, revenue claims, staking products, or promises of price appreciation.
