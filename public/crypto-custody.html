What Not To Lose

  1. Phantom recovery phrase, written offline.
  2. Mint authority keypair JSON or its original seed phrase, stored offline.
  3. The encryption passphrase used for encrypted USB secret bundles.
  4. A printed inventory with the public token mint, treasury wallet, mint authority, and backup date.

USB Backup Pattern

Keep three physical sets: one primary cold USB, one secondary cold USB stored separately, and one offsite USB or paper packet.

Store encrypted secret bundles, paper recovery phrases, paper encryption passphrases, and the public-address inventory. Never store an unencrypted seed phrase, keypair JSON, screenshot, or cloud note on a connected computer.

How To Manage Supply

  • Distribute existing tokens by transferring from a wallet that holds `$CR`.
  • Mint new tokens only through the mint authority or an approved game claim flow.
  • Move authority to a hardware wallet or multisig before any high-value treasury exists.
  • Revoke mint authority only after the economy and supply policy are final.
