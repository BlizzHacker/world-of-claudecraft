Knowledge Summaries

Short reference summaries. The Game Knowledge cards above open the complete guide pages.

Quick Start. Pick a realm from the home page, create a character and class, then enter. Move with WASD, look with the mouse, attack with left click, and toggle first-person view with V. Your character and progress are saved server-side per realm.
Combat. Combat is server-authoritative. Both a first-person (reticle-aimed) and third-person camera are supported — switch with V. Damage, hit detection, and cooldowns are resolved on the server; the client renders the result. Loot drops follow the rarity tiers described in Gameplay Systems.
Gameplay Systems. Level up through XP, then prestige for lifetime standing. Loot uses Diablo-style rarity tiers, and the pickit filter lets you auto-flag the items you care about. Platinum is an optional premium layer for cosmetics and the Exchange realm; the base game is free.
Parties. Group with other players to share XP and clear dungeons together. Party members appear on your map; rewards scale with the group.
The Gravecaller Saga. The central storyline threading the realms together. Read the full saga in the wiki when it is online.
Development Timeline. Cryptic Realm evolved from an upstream classic-MMO engine through the realm registry, dashboards, OIDC SSO, and the current alpha/beta tester cadence. Public shared-work notes live on Contributions.
$CR and platinum systems are optional utility layers. The game remains free to play, and wallet features must never require seed phrases, private keys, or keypair JSON from players.
